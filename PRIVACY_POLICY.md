# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 21, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how HR Grievances Bot ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise processes personal information in connection with our services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, employee ID, and other identification details
- **Grievance Information**: Details provided when submitting, updating, or commenting on grievances
- **Communication Data**: Messages, emails, and other communications related to grievances
- **Profile Information**: Department, role, manager information, and organizational hierarchy

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data**: How you interact with our application, features accessed, and time spent
- **Device Information**: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers
- **Log Data**: Server logs, access times, and error information
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies**: Session identifiers and tracking data

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **HR Systems**: Employee data synced from your organization's HR management systems
- **Organizational Data**: Department structures, reporting hierarchies, and organizational information

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Process and manage grievance submissions and resolutions
- Communicate with you regarding your grievances
- Improve and optimize our services
- Maintain system security and prevent fraud
- Comply with legal obligations
- Generate analytics and reports (in aggregated, anonymized form)
- Provide customer support
- Enforce our Terms and Conditions

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 4.1 We May Share Your Information With:
- **HR Personnel**: Relevant HR staff members designated to handle grievances
- **Management**: Appropriate managers as required for grievance resolution
- **Legal Representatives**: If required by law or to enforce agreements
- **Service Providers**: Third-party vendors who assist in operating our service (under confidentiality obligations)
- **Legal Authorities**: When required by law, court order, or government request

### 4.2 We Do NOT:
- Sell your personal data to third parties
- Share data with unrelated organizations without consent
- Use data for marketing purposes without explicit permission

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- Encryption of data in transit and at rest
- Access controls and authentication protocols
- Regular security audits and assessments
- Employee training on data protection
- Incident response procedures

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Grievance Data**: Retained for the duration of the grievance process plus a legally compliant period for records (typically 3-7 years)
- **User Account Data**: Retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward
- **Log Data**: Typically retained for 90 days unless longer retention is required
- **Deleted Data**: Securely deleted or anonymized upon request, unless legal holds apply

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- **Access**: Request access to your personal information
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a portable format
- **Opt-Out**: Withdraw consent for certain uses of your data
- **Restrict Processing**: Request limitation of how your data is used

To exercise these rights, contact us at the information provided in Section 11.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information promptly.

## 9. International Data Transfers

If your data is transferred across borders, we implement appropriate safeguards such as:
- Standard contractual clauses
- Privacy Shield compliance (where applicable)
- Adequate assurances of protection

## 10. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy and updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of our service constitutes acceptance of changes.
